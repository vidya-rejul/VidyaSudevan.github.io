About

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Computer and Information Engineering at Khalifa University. I earned a PhD in Engineering, specializing in Robotics, from Khalifa University in May 2025. My dissertation, Dehazing Techniques for Navigation in Underwater Environments, examined underwater visual perception for robot navigation.

Research appointments

Before my PhD, I worked as a Research Assistant and Research Associate at Khalifa University on perception and control for unmanned systems. My current work includes spiking neural networks for perception, semantic 3D underwater scene analysis, and visual–haptic interfaces for remote robot operation.

Project leadership

I developed the proposals for ReefSight3D and the Khalifa University–Qatar University visual–haptics project, and I lead both projects. I also co-supervise two PhD candidates in Robotics.
